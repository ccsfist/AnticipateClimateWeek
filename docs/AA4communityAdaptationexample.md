### Potential value of Anticipatory Action: making adaptation more sustainable

Adaptation can be vulnerable to climate risk

Consider an agroforestry project, with government and donor investments, and a community of farmers dedicating 30% of their farmland land for the forest.

In a normal year, the community gets their agricultural yields, as well as sustainable products from the agroforestry.

In a bad year, agroforestry products can help them manage decreased yields.

In a very bad year, they may need more than the forest can offer, overextracting from the forest and making it unsustainable.

Could Anticipatory Action before the forest is overextracted help make the forest sustainable?

Instead of simply providing a study, leadership, communities and experts can work together to see what adds up, in a simple framework that can be compared against what is realistic, to see how far things can be pushed before they stop working.
