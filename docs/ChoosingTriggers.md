# Choosing Trigger Rules Requires Disaster Decisionmakers

* It is challenging to choose trigger rules

* There are tough trade-offs between time, accuracy, budgets, and regrets

* The people who are responsible for disaster preparedness are responsible whether the trigger works or not

* They must make informed decisions about what to delegate to the math of predetermined Anticipatory Action Trigger Rules
