# Teachers College and the National Center for Disaster Preparedness in the Columbia University Climate school

## Real world education and real world action

We welcome you to this event.
Today we will be utilizing many of the active learning and gamification strategies utilized in our classes and in our projects.  

These strategies are not only used for trainings, but to work together to make progress on challenging practical issues.
